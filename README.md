# TEMIS · Virtual legal clinic with generative AI

Clear legal guidance when you don't know where to start.

**Website:** https://lauras-salazar.github.io/TEMIS/

TEMIS is a virtual legal clinic for people who have a legal problem but don't know what to do or where to go. The user tells their story in plain words; TEMIS asks follow-up questions, checks current Colombian law (RAG over verified legal sources) and gives initial guidance: steps, entity and documents. When the case needs representation, it connects the user with a specialised lawyer or partner firm.

Edit `assets/app.js` → `TEMIS_CONFIG` to add the video link, the research PDF link and the contact email.
